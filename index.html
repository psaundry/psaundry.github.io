Happy Strappers

Ten races, a full card, and plenty of happy strappers parading their beautifully presented horses into the mounting yard. Members and partners filled the room, and it felt like spring racing at its best.

A lot of fun.

In the room

Personalities

Harry Coffey

Harry rode in race 2 on Bohemian Boy for Troy Corstens and Will Larkin. He didn’t get the chocolates — but it was a pleasure to have the Swan Hill local in the room.

Will Larkin

Will was in the room representing Malua Racing for head trainer Troy Corstens. Malua’s unsung hero Leon was recognised in another room on course for all of his Caulfield Group 1’s. Troy couldn’t make it, but I am sure we will see him at an M&P function soon. It goes without saying Troy and Will are always looking for good owners if interested please reach out — maluaracing.com.au/horse-available.

Matt Hyland

CEO of the Victorian Jockeys Association. The sort of presence that makes a race day feel like a race day. The Hyland name is synonymous with racing.

The namesake

Might and Power

Bay gelding, 1993 · Zabeel – Benediction · Jack Denham

Caulfield Cup, Melbourne Cup, Cox Plate. His performances on the track gave pleasure to many of the members who named the club.

Thank you

MRCMelbourne Racing Club

It was wonderful to see so many members’ partners enjoying a day at the races. Ten presentations, a yard full of good horses, and personalities on the floor. A special day for the club — at Caulfield, with Melbourne Racing Club.

Best wishes to you all in the spring!
