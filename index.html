Might and Power Club · Industry Support Race Day

Saturday 19 September 2026

A very good day.

Ten tables walked into the mounting yard to present the strapper prize. Members and partners, a full card, and a room that felt like racing at its best. It was a lot of fun.

Hosted at Caulfield with Melbourne Racing Club

MRC Melbourne Racing Club

A big thank you to Stu and Pete for putting it together — and to every table that scored, walked down, and made a fuss of the people who do the work every morning.

One phone per table. Score while the horses are in the yard. Present as they walk to the start. Two hundred and fifty dollars a race, five hundred on the Group 1s. Supporting the industry, in the nicest possible way.

In the room

Personalities

Harry Coffey

Harry rode in race 2 on Bohemian Boy for Troy Corstens and Will Larkin. Will came up to speak; Troy couldn’t make it. Harry didn’t get the chocolates — but it was a pleasure to have him in the room.

Matt Hyland

Matt was with us representing the jockeys association — son of legend Pat Hyland. The sort of presence that makes a race day feel like a race day.

The namesake

Might and Power

Bay gelding, 1993 · Zabeel – Benediction · Jack Denham

No official link with the great Australian horse — only the original inspiration. Caulfield Cup, Melbourne Cup, Cox Plate. His performances on the track gave pleasure to many of the members who named the club.

Thank you

MRC Melbourne Racing Club

It was wonderful to see so many members’ partners enjoying a day at the races. Ten presentations, a yard full of good horses, and personalities on the floor. A special day for the club — at Caulfield, with Melbourne Racing Club.
